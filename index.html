About Me

Retention that keeps users coming back.

I own retention marketing for 5 brands — Horocosmo, Gemsmantra, Aap, Poojan & Occult Gurukul. That means writing daily push notification copy for Horocosmo and planning marketing campaigns across WhatsApp, email & SMS for every brand, end to end.

I don't just write the copy — I build the creatives for each campaign and execute them on ground across every channel. Once a campaign is live, I track it: ROAS, ROI, spend & revenue, so every send is judged on what it actually delivers.

Before retention, I wrote sports content for Sportskeeda — that background in writing for an audience is exactly what shows up in every push notification and campaign line I write today.
